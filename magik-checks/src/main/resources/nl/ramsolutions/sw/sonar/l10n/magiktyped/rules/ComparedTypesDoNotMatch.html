Compared types do not match

Explanation

_method a.m(param1)
    ## @param {sw:integer} param1
    _if param1 _is _unset
    _then
        # This condition will never be true, since param1 is defined as sw:integer,
        # and can therefore never be sw:unset.
    _endif
_endmethod
$

In the above example, we are trying to compare two different types: 

sw:integer
and 
sw:unset
. Given that the type definition of 
param1
is 
sw:integer
, it can never be 
sw:unset
.
