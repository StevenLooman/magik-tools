Fix "Go to Implementation" ignoring the method name under the cursor

Implementations were gathered from every subtype of the exemplar without regard for the method name,
so an abstract method answered with all methods of all its descendants. The method under the cursor
is now matched by name, its assignment form included, and only that method's own `_abstract`
modifier decides whether it has implementations. A method invocation no longer answers with the
implementations of the method it appears in.
