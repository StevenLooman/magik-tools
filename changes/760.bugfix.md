Consider every colliding definition instead of picking one.

A name with several definitions, such as a procedure also assigned through a global,
a global assigned two exemplars, or an exemplar defined by two modules, used to
resolve to whichever definition happened to come first. The result could differ
depending on where the checkout was located.

Type reasoning now takes all definitions into account: parents, `is_kind_of?`, and
responding methods and procedures are the union over every definition. Typed checks
only report on a collision what holds for every definition, and the language server
shows every definition.
