Record the usages in a global's initializing expression, and record operator usages.

`GlobalDefinition` now has `usedMethods`, `usedGlobals` and `usedBinaryOperators`.
`MethodDefinition` has `usedBinaryOperators` added, unary operators are recorded as method usages.
New settings `magik.typing.indexUnaryOperatorUsages` and
`magik.typing.indexBinaryOperatorUsages` are added to gate the extraction.
