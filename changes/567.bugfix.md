Flag missing pragmas on top-level definitional method invocations.

`MissingPragma` now reports an issue when a top-level statement invokes
`.add_child()`, `.define_shared_constant()`, or `.define_shared_variable()`
without a preceding pragma.

Only statements at file top level are checked. Invocations inside a `_method`,
`_proc`, or `_block` body are treated as regular method calls.
